Atomic Structure as a Directed Hypergraph

Evidence graph of Scilib.AtomicStructure, formalized in Lean 4

October 21, 2026

AbstractWe encode the evidence for three models of the atom as a directed hypergraph, where each hyperedge maps a set of premises to a set of conclusions. A world is a set of theories and probes; the graph then assigns every experiment a verdict of supported, refuted, or silent. Choose a world below, and hover any element for its Lean statement.

1 The evidence graph

Figure 1: Directed hypergraph of the evidence structure. Hollow squares are implies hyperedges (theory → claim); filled squares are interprets hyperedges ({claim, probe} → experiment). Dashed colored lines show each world’s member theories and probes; the red dashed line is a contradiction, which is not a directed edge.
