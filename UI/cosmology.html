Cosmology as an Evidence Graph

Evidence graph of Scilib.Cosmology, written in Lean 4

October 21, 2026

AbstractModern cosmology evolved through two major paradigm shifts: first, from Einstein’s static universe to a dynamic expanding model following Hubble’s redshift law; second, from a matter-dominated decelerating expansion to an accelerating universe driven by dark energy. This page lays out, test by test, how static and decelerating models were refuted by galactic redshifts and Type Ia supernovae, while LambdaCDM remains supported across all probes. Pick a world below, and hover or tap any box for details.

1 The evidence graph

Figure 1: One row for each cosmological probe. On the left is what the model predicts. The red dashed line means that prediction and the observation below it cannot both be true. The LambdaCDM prediction underneath explains the observation. The experiment is on the right, with its verdict for each world in words. Small coloured dots on a box show which worlds include it: blue for Static, teal for Decelerating, orange for LambdaCDM. Faded boxes are not part of the world you selected.

Table 1: Verdict for each experiment. “Supported” means the world leads to what was observed. “Refuted” means the world predicts something the experiment contradicts. “Silent” means the world says nothing about it. The selected world is shaded.
