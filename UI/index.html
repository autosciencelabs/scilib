SciLib · Lean 4

SciLib Examples

Scilib, a verifier for progressively-formalized scientific reasoning, models it as a hypergraph: theories and observations connect through explicit evidence, predictions, and contradictions. These interactive examples show how competing scientific explanations fare against experiments; the evidence models are formalized with Lean 4.

Explore the examples

  • Atomic structureExplore atomic theory and experimental evidence as a directed hypergraph.
  • Beta decayCompare the classical two-body prediction with the neutrino explanation of the continuous spectrum.
  • Bernoulli’s principleSee how ideal-flow predictions compare with observations and a real-flow model.
  • Bragg’s lawFollow the evidence for crystal diffraction and compare crystal and amorphous worlds.
  • CosmologyCompare cosmological models against observations from multiple probes.
  • Light diffractionExplore how competing optics theories explain diffraction experiments.
  • Rainbow, soap film & Magnus effectThree interactive hypergraphs for rainbows, thin-film colours, and spinning bodies in flow.
  • SuperconductivityCompare resistance, London phenomenology, and BCS theory against experiments.
