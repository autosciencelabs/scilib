1 The evidence graph

Figure 1: Directed hypergraph of the argument. Each small square is a hyperedge: hollow squares are interprets (experiment → reading), filled squares are entails (all premises → conclusion). Faded elements are not derived from the current granted set. Red dashed lines are contradictions; a red square is a nogood set of three or more. A red outline marks a conflict, and a red experiment is refuted.
