Evidence graph of Scilib.Superconductivity, written in Lean 4

AbstractSuperconductivity brings electrical resistance down to zero and expels magnetic fields. While a simple phenomenological or resistance-only model accounts for basic zero-resistance drops, the full microscopic BCS theory and electron-phonon coupling model explain magnetic flux expulsion, isotope effects, and specific heat gaps. Explore each test, what models predict, and how background worlds handle them.

1 The evidence graph

Figure 1: One row for each experiment. On the left is what the background theory predicts. The experiment is on the right, with its verdict for each world in words. Small coloured dots on a box show which worlds include it: blue for pure resistance, teal for London phenomenological, and orange for full BCS theory.
